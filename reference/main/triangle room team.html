The room

There was a small triangle shaped room in Trivandrum. Anu lived and worked in it while he was learning to edit, and then all the way through college at Mar Ivanios.

Midhuna and Jibin were his classmates. The three of them used to assemble in that room to make things, and to talk about the things they wanted to make one day. Almost none of it got made, because nobody had any money and nobody was giving three students a commission.

Then everyone left for work and spent fifteen years learning the business from inside other people's shows. The company is named after the room because that is where it actually started.

The Partners

Corner 01
Anu Kamala
Director
Story and Post

Anu believes stories are rewritten in the edit room. He has spent eighteen years there, cutting documentary series, reality formats, travel shows and live sport for Netflix, National Geographic, Discovery, Olympic Channel, Prime Video and Star Sports.

In unscripted, that has never only meant cutting. Finding the arc inside three hundred hours of rushes, restructuring episodes after the shoot is over, rewriting a series so it holds. On most of his projects that work has been creative producing without the credit, which is the ordinary arrangement in non-fiction and the reason he now wants to develop his own.

He was series editor on The Greatest Rivalry for Netflix, chief editor on Doubles Trouble for Olympic Channel, and film editor on Tarini: The Goddess and the Sea for National Geographic. Across eighteen years the work has run regional, national and international, across languages and across formats: India From Above for National Geographic in the UK, The Greatest 2 Minutes in Sports for the US market, social content cut in Paris for Jellysmack and in London for Glassworks. He now develops and directs original work at Triangle Room.

Corner 02
Jibin Babu
Director
Development and Writing

Jibin writes the thing before anyone knows it is a thing. Over nine years across television, audio, film and digital content, he has developed and led production on more than twelve television shows, including Music Mojo Season 7 for Kappa TV, Autumn Leaf The Big Stage, and reality formats for Amrita TV.

His own work runs to fiction. He has written and directed audio series for Pratilipi FM and co-created short films, mini web series and ad films. He is currently co-writing a feature screenplay.

Corner 03
Midhuna Pichy
Director
Production and Delivery

Midhuna makes ambitious shows actually happen. Eleven years producing television, reality and ad film across Mumbai, Hyderabad, Chennai and Kerala. She has worked three seasons of Bigg Boss Malayalam for Asianet and Endemol Shine India, moving from episode producer on Season 1 to post producer on Season 3 to creative producer on Season 4, alongside seven other international reality formats and ad films for HP, Platinum and Exide.

Her specialism is the daily reality pipeline. Twenty four hours of rushes cut down to two, then to one hour of prime time, delivered on schedule every single day. It is a job of making the right call continuously under pressure, and of running a team so those calls get made and the show goes out.

Her earlier work runs through Big Synergy Media Ltd on Kaun Banega Crorepati, Dus Ka Dum, People's Choice, Sell Me The Answer and Fastest Family First, and through MTV and Endemol Shine India on Ace of Space. She has developed shows for the south region at Studio Next, Sony Pictures.

How the room works

Story first

Every project starts as a story problem, not a format problem. If it does not hold in one sentence, it does not go into development.

Built to deliver

Between the three of us we have run development, live production and post on shows at broadcast scale. We pitch what we know we can make.

Kerala, not limited to it

Our stories come from where we are from. The audience does not have to.

Our note to you

In the heart of every great story lies a meeting point of minds, of moments, of meaning. Triangle Room was born at such a point.

Three college mates turned storytellers. The triangle is the bond: three corners, each unique, each part of one strong shape. The room is where it begins, the brainstorming, the chaos, the creation.

We are here to craft films that are not just seen but felt. Stories that are rooted yet boundless, local yet universal.

Welcome to the room where imagination finds its edge.

Our vision

To make work that lingers long after the credits roll. Stories that challenge, comfort and change the way we see the world.

Bold in form. Rich in feeling. Timeless in impact.

We do not just make films. We carve them.
