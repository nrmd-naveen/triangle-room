Who we are

Before it became a production studio, it was a small, triangular room in Trivandrum. It served as Anu's home and workspace while he mastered editing throughout his years at Mar Ivanios College.

It was in that room that Anu, Midhuna and Jibin first came together, ideating, experimenting and mapping out the work they wanted to create. While few of those early projects were ever released, the foundation of a shared creative voice was built.

Their careers soon expanded into key sectors of the media landscape.

AnuRelocated to Mumbai as a post-production editor, cutting non-fiction and documentary for Netflix, National Geographic, Discovery, Olympic Channel, JioHotstar and Prime Video. Sport, food, reality, motoring, travel: the range that made production houses bring him back project after project.
MidhunaManaged major production pipelines across Mumbai, Hyderabad, Chennai and Kerala, running high-volume reality television and commercial campaigns for Sony, Asianet, MTV and Endemol Shine India.
JibinFocused on development in Kerala, directing and writing across television, narrative audio and short film for Kappa TV, Wonderwall Media and Amrita TV, alongside a large body of work made for YouTube.

Fifteen years later, the three founders have reunited as directors of Triangle Room. Named after the space where it all began, the company combines three careers of cross-industry expertise, scale and technical capability with the same original instinct: to bring ambitious ideas to completion.
